# Hypnosis

*Look at the watch... left... and right...*

A golden pocket watch hangs from the top of the screen and swings back and forth while you play. The longer you survive, the deeper your **trance** gets:

- The level slowly sways along with the watch and gently breathes in and out.
- Calm suggestions fade in: *"You are getting very sleepy..."*, *"You will not miss the next orb..."*
- The watch glows to the beat of the music, and its hands tick along with the level time.

When you die, the hypnotist **snaps their fingers**. You wake up and the trance starts over.

Settings: watch opacity and color, swing speed, time to reach full trance, camera sway strength (0 turns it off), music glow, and suggestions.
